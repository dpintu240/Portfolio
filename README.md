# Pintu Das - Personal Research Portfolio

Welcome to the official repository of my personal academic and professional portfolio website. I am a PhD Research Scholar specializing in Fluvial Geomorphology and Multi-Hazard Risk Modeling.

* **Programming & Documentation:** RStudio (`ggplot2`, `corrplot`), Python, SPSS, LaTeX.
